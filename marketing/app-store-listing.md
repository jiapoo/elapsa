**Description**
```
Your passport expires six months before it says it does.

Many countries won't let you board or enter with less than six months left on your passport, and renewing one can take weeks. Elapsa tells you when to START renewing, timed to how long each renewal really takes, not when it's already too late.

No subscription. One purchase. Nothing leaves your phone.

SCAN IT, DON'T TYPE IT
• Hold a document up to the camera. Elapsa outlines it as you hold it, crops it straight and reads it.
• Passports, visas and ID cards: the machine-readable zone is read field by field and checked against its check digits, so what's saved is what's printed.
• Cards without one, such as Chinese ID cards, driving licences and bank cards: Elapsa reads the printed expiry date, number, name and issuer, even a silver-foil number on a patterned card.
• It recognises Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Cyrillic, Thai, Arabic, Vietnamese and Latin print by itself. Anything it isn't sure of is left for you to check, never guessed.

REMINDED IN TIME
• Each kind of document has its own renewal lead time: a passport months ahead, a membership a few weeks.
• "96 days left · start renewing now". Every passport is flagged when it falls below the six-month travel rule.
• Daily reminders in the final month. Reminders arrive even if you never open the app.

ONE WALLET FOR EVERYTHING
• Passports, ID cards, visas, residence permits, driving licences, vehicle registrations, insurance, professional licences, memberships and bank cards, plus types you name yourself.
• Front and back photos, your own fields, attached files and links.
• Search and sort, and show names only when someone's looking over your shoulder.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No server. No analytics.
• Documents and photos are encrypted on your iPhone.
• Moving to a new phone? Send everything directly to it nearby, or as one encrypted file by AirDrop.

FREE FOR 3 DOCUMENTS
Track up to three documents free. Unlock unlimited documents with a single one-time purchase. No subscription, nothing renews.

Elapsa speaks English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Português, Italiano and Español.

Entry rules vary by country. Always check the rules of the country you're travelling to.
```

## App Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

```
Elapsa stores everything on the device. There is no account and no sign-in, so no demo account is needed.

To see the in-app purchase: add three documents with the + button (any name and date), then save a fourth. The paywall appears. Restore Purchase is on the paywall and in Settings → Elapsa Unlimited.

To try scanning without a real document, choose "Choose a photo" on the scan screen and pick any photo of a passport or card.

Elapsa has no server. The only network use is the App Store purchase (handled by StoreKit) and the optional direct transfer to the user's own nearby phone over the local network.
```

Also set: Sign-in required → **No**. Contact: your name, phone and email (Apple only).
