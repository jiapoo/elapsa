# App Review notes for Elapsa 1.0 (resubmission)

Paste into App Store Connect → version 1.0 → App Review Information → Notes. The screen
recording goes in the Attachment field below the notes.

```
Screen recording: attached. Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at launch and shows scanning a passport, the wallet and reminders, the paywall on the fourth document, the purchase, and Restore Purchase after reinstalling.

1. Purpose and audience
Elapsa tracks when passports, visas, ID cards and other documents expire, and tells people when to START renewing. Many countries refuse entry with less than six months left on a passport, and renewal takes weeks, so the printed date is too late to act on. The app is for travellers, expats and families who hold several documents. Everything stays on the device.

2. How to use it
No account, no sign-in, no demo credentials needed.
- Tap + and choose Scan to read a passport, visa or ID card with the camera. To try without a real document, choose "Choose a photo" on the scan screen and pick any photo of a passport or card.
- Or tap + and type a name and expiry date by hand.
- The wallet shows days left; tap a document to see its reminders.
- In-app purchase: three documents are free. Saving a fourth shows the paywall ("Elapsa Unlimited", one-time, non-consumable). Restore Purchase is on the paywall and in Settings → Elapsa Unlimited.

3. External services
Only Apple's StoreKit, for the one-time in-app purchase. There is no server, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK that sends data, and no AI service. Text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. The optional "Transfer to a nearby phone" feature talks directly to the user's own second device over the local network.

4. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in every country where it is offered.

5. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. Elapsa does not issue, verify or submit documents to any authority; it only stores the user's own records on their device.
```
